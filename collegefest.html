Tech fest 2026

About Event

TechFest 2026 is the annual college festival that brings together students from different departments to participate in technical, cultural, and fun activities. The event includes coding contests, quizzes, workshops, and cultural performances.

Event Schedule

  1. 09:00 AM - Registration
  2. 10:00 AM - Opening Ceremony
  3. 11:00 AM - Coding Competition
  4. 01:00 PM - Lunch Break
  5. 02:00 PM - Technical Quiz
  6. 04:00 PM - Cultural Performances
  7. 06:00 PM - Prize Distribution

Rules

  • All participants must carry their college ID card.
  • Registration is mandatory.
  • Maintain discipline throughout the event.
  • Any form of cheating will lead to disqualification.
  • Follow instructions given by event coordinators.
